# Illustrations de la présentation

Créées avec l’outil intégré imagegen. Ce sont des illustrations pédagogiques générées, pas des photographies du site ni des dessins techniques validés.

## Accueil

Fichier : [illustration_accueil.png](./illustration_accueil.png)

Prompt final :

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial illustration for a French academic project defense PowerPoint, white-background slide. Create a refined contemporary editorial illustration of a new employee in a clean automotive wiring harness factory, looking at a tablet with an abstract chat interface, with a trainer beside them. A neatly arranged wiring harness and connector assembly on an inactive workbench, subtle cable reels and industrial equipment in the background. Style: premium soft 3D / editorial digital illustration, sophisticated friendly geometric shapes, realistic proportions, minimal fine detail, white background blending to pure white edges. Palette: navy blue, teal, pale mint, with a tiny warm amber accent. Composition: landscape 3:2, focus on the two people, tablet and wiring harness; generous breathing room. Purpose: educational onboarding assistant, not a real factory photo. No writing, no letters, no digits, no logos, no watermark, no large robot, no holograms, no dense decorations. Calm professional mood, bright soft studio light.

## Faisceau

Fichier : [illustration_faisceau.png](./illustration_faisceau.png)

Prompt final :

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: illustration for a French academic PowerPoint about onboarding employees in an automotive wiring factory. Create a clean premium 3D editorial illustration of an automotive wiring harness laid out in an elegant branching shape, with dark grey corrugated protective sleeves, several black electrical connectors and a few visible teal, blue and amber wires. Beside it show a simple small cable reel and a neatly cut wire with metal crimp terminal, to suggest production stages. No tools in operation, no hands, no machinery controls. Pure white background with subtle soft shadows, professional educational composition, generous white margin. Landscape 3:2. Navy and teal palette with tiny amber accents, consistent with a bright white engineering slide deck. Not an engineering drawing, illustrative only. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, dimensions, watermark, decorative UI or robot.
